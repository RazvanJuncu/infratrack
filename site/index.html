What this does NOT tell us

  • That detected changes are definitely roadworks.
  • The percentage of construction completed.
  • Anything about small road markings or internal structures.
  • Full coverage of the 8.6 km A0 lot.

The analysis window is now centered on an OSM-mapped A0 Lot 3 segment. This is a map-derived corridor, not an authoritative engineering alignment. Crop harvesting and seasonal vegetation can still cause false detections. The road buffer is 130 m, not the legal construction footprint.
