Errors and limitations

—

Spectral screening compares NDVI and reflectance from real Sentinel-2 scenes. Clouds, seasonal variation and 10–20 m spatial resolution limit interpretation. On-demand previews for personally saved areas run in your browser and are not processed by this server pipeline.
